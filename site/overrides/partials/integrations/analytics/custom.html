{# PostHog web analytics (Material's "custom" analytics provider, enabled by extra.analytics in mkdocs.yml), strictly after consent. - Nothing loads before consent: no PostHog script, no request, nothing stored. EU rules (ePrivacy Art. 5(3), in Spain LSSI-CE Art. 22.2, read with EDPB Guidelines 2/2023) treat a tracking script as access to the device even without cookies, so even cookieless counting waits for "Accept". The banner is partials/consent.html. - "Accept" loads PostHog on the spot: its ph__posthog cookie (365 days, this host only) and session replay with every input masked. "Reject" opts out if PostHog is already running and deletes its cookies and storage (purge()); PostHog never loads again for that visitor. - The choice is ours to remember: gg_consent in localStorage holds {v, choice, at}, strictly necessary. It is void after 365 days or when consent_version in mkdocs.yml changes (bump it whenever legal/cookies.md changes what we set), and the banner asks again. Until a new "Accept", any old PostHog cookie or storage is purged on load. "Cookie settings" in the footer reopens the banner. - Never call identify(): visitors stay anonymous even with a cookie. - Production only: runs on gitgrit.dev, not staging or local mkdocs serve. before_send is a second guard and drops every event unless the hostname is still that host, so nothing is sent from localhost, 127.0.0.1, staging, or www. Off production the banner never shows. - api_host is PostHog's managed reverse proxy on our own subdomain (extra.analytics.api_host). The loader fetches the SDK from /static/array.js. - Named events sit alongside autocapture (contact_clicked, app_clicked, get_started_clicked, github_clicked, demo_clicked). demo_booked fires from the Cal.com embed (partials/cal-embed.html) when a booking completes. - Pricing page (docs/pricing.md): every CTA click above also carries `plan` (free / team / business / enterprise, from data-plan), `billing_period` (monthly / annual) and `currency` (usd / eur; European time zones start on eur). Plus pricing_period_toggled {period}, pricing_currency_toggled {currency}, and pricing_section_viewed {section} once per section per page load: plans, enterprise, faq, and each FAQ question (faq:). Scroll depth and time on page come from PostHog's $pageleave; heatmaps show where people click and hover. Mailto values are stored as mailto: plus the subject, never the address. navigation.instant is off, so one delegated listener per full page load is enough. - EU cloud. The project key is a public, write-only browser key. - Loader stub: PostHog's official snippet (posthog.com/docs/libraries/js). #}
