{# Book a demo: Cal.com modal, loaded on the first click only. Any element with data-cal-link="gitgrit-demo/30min" (optionally data-cal-config='{...}') opens the booking modal. Give it href="https://cal.com/gitgrit-demo/30min" too: the plain booking page is the fallback when JS is off.
