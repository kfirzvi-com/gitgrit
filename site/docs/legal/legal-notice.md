## Reporting illegal content

To report content on gitgrit.dev or in the GitGrit service (for example, a public Marketplace standard) that you believe is illegal, email [kfir@praktical.dev](mailto:kfir@praktical.dev) with the subject "Illegal content report" and include:

1. why you consider the content illegal;
2. the exact URL, or enough detail to locate it;
3. your name and email address (not required for reports about child sexual abuse material);
4. a statement that you believe, in good faith, that the report is accurate and complete.

We confirm receipt, review each report diligently and without undue delay, and tell you what we decided and whether you can challenge it. If we restrict someone's content or account, we explain why to them, and they can contest the decision by replying to that email. We do not use automated tools to make these decisions.
