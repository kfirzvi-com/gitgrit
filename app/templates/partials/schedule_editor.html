A schedule is a cron expression, built like a filter search: type it (the box suggests values for the field the caret is in), or pick each field from its menu, or start from a preset. Under it, the schedule in words and its next times, read by the server (schedule_preview). The schedule's time zone is picked here and belongs to it: everyone reads its times in that zone (the reading adds the viewer's own times when theirs differs). Apply writes the rows' trigger-/schedule-/schedule_tz- fields; the table's Save saves them. Expects `trigger_options`.{% endcomment %}
